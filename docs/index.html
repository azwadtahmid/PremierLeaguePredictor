How it works

Scorelines. A goals model gives every team an attack and a defence rating, learned from past results with recent matches counting more. From those it estimates each side's expected goals, and from that the chance of every scoreline. The predicted score is the most likely scoreline for the most likely result.

Predicted table. After every matchweek, the rest of the season is played out 10,000 times with random scores drawn from the goals model. Counting where each team finishes gives its chance of the title, the top 4 and relegation. These chances are probably too confident, because the simulation treats team strengths as fixed.

Honest scoring. Each prediction is locked about 48 hours before kick-off and never edited. A right result scores 1 point, an exact score 3. The benchmarks are "always pick the home team" and the Bet365 favourite.
